# Reference Provenance — Rocket League
All reference material must be lawfully obtained. No Psyonix assets redistributed.

## Allowed
- Your own screen captures of RL running on your hardware (video, screenshots)
- Links to official Psyonix/Epic trailers (YouTube) with URL + date accessed
- Notes on observed behavior (written descriptions are for internal notes only, not as substitute for blind A/B)

## Not Allowed
- Ripped models, textures, audio, pak files
- Redistributed gameplay video in repo without rights

## Log Format
| Date | Source | Hardware | RL Version | Capture | Used for WS | Lawful basis |
|------|--------|----------|------------|---------|-------------|--------------|
| YYYY-MM-DD | e.g. PS5 gameplay own capture | PS5 | vX.XX | `reference/captures/2026-08-30_kickoff.mp4` (local only, not committed if copyrighted) | WS59 | own capture, fair use for benchmarking, not redistributed |

Note: Large captures stay local and are NOT committed. Only this log and derived measurements (timings, curves) are committed.
